WEBUOS Global Business Search Engine

TAGLINE:
World Enterprises Business Unified Operating System Connecting & Powering Business.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE:

Transform the existing WEBUOS landing page into a powerful, enterprise-grade global search engine and business discovery platform.

The search experience should be inspired by the usability and simplicity of Google Search, but WEBUOS must NOT be a visual clone of Google.

WEBUOS is specifically designed for BUSINESS DISCOVERY.

Users must be able to search and discover:

• Companies
• Businesses
• Products
• Services
• Industries
• Technologies
• AI companies
• Manufacturers
• Suppliers
• Distributors
• Wholesalers
• Consultants
• Professional service providers
• Business categories
• Industrial organizations
• IT companies
• AI companies
• Commercial businesses
• Business professionals
• Business locations
• Business opportunities

The existing WEBUOS landing page is the primary visual reference and must be preserved as the design language.

8. SEARCH RESULT RELEVANCE ENGINE
==================================================

Implement a relevance-ranking system.

Ranking signals should include:

1. Exact query match
2. Entity match
3. Search intent match
4. Category match
5. Industry match
6. Location match
7. Business verification
8. Profile completeness
9. Product/service relevance
10. Text relevance
11. Popularity
12. Freshness
13. User interaction signals
14. Business quality signals

Do NOT simply sort alphabetically.

The system should calculate a relevance score internally.

Example:

relevance_score =
query_match +
entity_match +
category_match +
location_match +
industry_match +
quality_score +
freshness_score

The actual internal score does not need to be displayed to users.

• Prefix search
• Typo tolerance
• Fuzzy matching
• Filters
• Facets
• Ranking
• Geo search
• Synonyms
• Autocomplete

The implementation must remain replaceable so the search engine provider can be changed later.
